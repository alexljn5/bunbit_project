### My Honest Take

The strongest direction right now seems to be something like:

> **"Exploring broken, liminal industrial facilities that are also metaphors for trauma, memory, and systems that failed people."**

This lets you use:
- Your love for facilities + liminal spaces
- The raycaster's claustrophobic, retro-tech feel
- Your dark ambient/drone/industrial music
- Subtle, sensitive themes without being preachy

The player could be navigating these spaces while slowly uncovering stories about people who got lost in the system (could tie into mentorship themes indirectly).
