# LORE.md — Bunbit Engine

> This is a living spitball document. Nothing is final. Throw ideas in here freely.

## Core Concept (Current Direction)

A melancholic, atmospheric exploration game set inside decaying, liminal industrial facilities. The player navigates broken systems — both physical and emotional — while slowly uncovering fragments of stories about people who got lost inside them.

The game leans into:
- Liminal dread and facility exploration
- Heavy ambient / industrial atmosphere
- Subtle emotional weight and quiet human moments
- A symbolic "corrupted bunny" entity that acts as a damaged guide/protector

Tone goal: Melancholic and introspective, with rare moments of strange tenderness. Not misery porn, not forced positivity.

---

## The World

### The Facilities
- Vast, decaying industrial/research complexes that feel half-abandoned, half-still-running on corrupted automation.
- Architecture is brutalist, cold, and repetitive. Long corridors, sterile rooms, observation decks, containment zones.
- Time and space feel unstable in places (liminal qualities). Some areas loop, some feel "remembered" rather than real.
- The deeper you go, the more the facility feels like it's bleeding into memory, trauma, and broken systems.

### The "Signal"
- Something is always running beneath the surface.
- Old systems, old experiments, old pain that never fully shut down.
- The rabbit never stops moving.

---

## The Bunny Entity

Current working idea: **A corrupted / damaged protector figure**.

- Not purely evil, not purely good.
- Represents damaged innocence, failed protection, and trying anyway.
- Can be unsettling, but also strangely gentle at times.
- Possibly fragments of multiple "versions" of the same entity (some more corrupted than others).
- Symbolic of the player’s own relationship with trauma, protection, and care (especially the mentorship side).

Possible questions to explore:
- What was the bunny originally meant to protect?
- Why did it become corrupted?
- Does it want to help the player, or is it just following old programming?
- Can it be "helped" in some way, or is that even the point?

---

## Themes & Emotional Core

### Primary Themes
- Broken systems (institutions, families, minds)
- Liminal spaces as emotional states
- Damaged protectors / corrupted caretakers
- Memory vs reality
- Quiet attempts at connection in cold places

### Emotional Tone
- Melancholic, lonely, atmospheric
- Occasional strange warmth or recognition (never loud)
- The game doesn’t force healing, but it doesn’t wallow either
- Small moments of humanity feel rare and valuable

---

## Possible Story Fragments / Things the Player Can Find

- Old logs and recordings from people who worked in the facility
- Notes left by people who tried to protect others and failed
- Echoes or fragments of younger versions of people (could tie into mentorship themes)
- The bunny entity reacting to certain areas or objects in subtle ways
- Areas that feel more "remembered" than real (emotional memory made physical)

---

## Music Direction

- Heavy ambient and dark drones as the core
- Industrial techno elements in more active/dangerous areas
- Occasional piano or slowcore moments for emotional beats
- Music should feel like it's coming from the facility itself (old systems still playing corrupted songs)

## Notes & Vibes

- The aesthetic is "industrial rabbit" — cold systems wrapped in something damaged and strangely personal.
- The game should feel like walking through someone else’s broken memory of a place.
- Small human moments should feel earned and fragile.
- The spinning sigil / occult imagery can stay symbolic rather than literal magic.
